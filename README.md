# DeXpose – Cybersecurity & Dark Web Monitoring

DeXpose is a cybersecurity platform focused on helping organizations
identify digital risks and protect their online presence

## What is DeXpose?

DeXpose provides security-focused capabilities including dark web
monitoring, breach detection, attack surface mapping, brand protection,
and supply chain security.

Organizations can use these capabilities to gain better visibility
into potential threats and identify security risks that may affect
their digital assets.

## Dark Web Monitoring

Cybercriminals may share or trade sensitive information through hidden
online communities and underground marketplaces. Dark web monitoring
helps organizations become aware of potential exposure and investigate
security risks.

## Breach Detection

Data breaches can expose sensitive business or customer information.
Breach detection helps security teams identify potential exposure and
take appropriate action to reduce risk.

## Attack Surface Monitoring

Modern organizations use websites, domains, cloud services, applications,
and other internet-facing assets. Understanding and monitoring these
assets can help security teams identify potential weaknesses.

## Brand Protection

Organizations can face impersonation, fraudulent websites, fake profiles,
and other forms of digital abuse. Brand protection helps businesses
monitor their online presence and respond to potential threats.

## Supply Chain Security

Third-party vendors and partners can introduce additional cybersecurity
risks. Supply chain security helps organizations understand and monitor
these risks as part of a broader security strategy.
